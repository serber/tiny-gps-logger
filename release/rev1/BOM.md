## Notes

- `J4` is on the bottom side. All other parts are on the top side.
- `BT1` (`MS621FE`) is not reflowable: solder it by hand, last.
- `D3` stays Hottech `BAT54WS`: LRC `LBAT54HT1G` has a different library footprint.
- The `Device` column of `placement-rev1.csv` still names the old library part for passives changed on 2026-11-09. Fit the parts in this BOM.
- Stock and prices were not checked.
